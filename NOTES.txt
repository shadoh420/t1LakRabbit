LakRabbit for Tribes 1.11 - notes for script authors

HOW THE PORT WORKS AROUND T1
- T1 has no parent::. The mod contains changed copies of stock functions.
- Shot distance and speed: T1 does not give the projectile start point.
  The mod keeps a position history of each player (10 samples each second).
- Mid-air test and height: GameBase::getLOSInfo with a pitch of 90 degrees down.
- Direct hit: T1 disc and plasma explode at the surface of the target.
  The mod uses the measured damage of a direct hit as 100%.
- Hand grenade and grenade launcher have the same damage type in T1.
  The mod records hand grenade explosions to find the weapon.

ENGINE BEHAVIOR (TRIBES 1.11)
- AI client ids do not keep script fields. Client::getFirst/getNext skip AI clients.
- buyItem stops the server when it is used for an AI client.
- Projectiles from AI players give damage source 0 to Player::onDamage.
- Vector::getRotation also sets a pitch. For a player, set the yaw only (forward is +y).
- The server writes ServerPrefs.cs when it stops, and it removes blank values.

TESTS
Copy lakrabbit and laktest into the Tribes folder, then run:
  powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File laktest\run_tests.ps1
